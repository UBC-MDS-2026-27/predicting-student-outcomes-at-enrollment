# DSCI 522 Group 5 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We strive to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming environment for everyone contributing to Group 5 for course DSCI 522 regardless of background, identity, disability, or experience level.

## Expected behaviour from group members

Contributors should:

- Communicate respectfully and consider different perspectives of others.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work rather than the person.
- Accept feedback thoughtfully and acknowledge mistakes rather than avoiding responsibility.
- Credit others' contributions and respect their time and boundaries.
- Show up to group meeting times scheduled and agreed by the group
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

We do not tolerate:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Insults or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions or collaboration.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, meetings, and other communications related to the project.

## Reporting concerns

Failure to follow the code of conduct will result in a three-step warning system:

1. A written Slack notice that outlines the violation of the conduct and reminds the member of the team contract and code of conduct conditions.
2. A meeting to discuss the behaviour, after which the member writes an agreement to follow the team contract and code of conduct.
3. Reaching out to the lab instructor or TAs about the issues with the teammate.


To report a concern privately, email Sky Kehan Sheng (Instructor)

message to `@Sky` in the course Slack workspace.

## Responding to concerns

The designated contact will acknowledge the report, review the available information, and consult an uninvolved team member or the course teaching team when appropriate.

Responses will depend on the seriousness and frequency of the behaviour.They may include requesting a change in behaviour, issuing a warning, moderating inappropriate content, or referring the matter to the instructor and TA. Any restrictions affecting assessed course participation will be coordinated with the teaching team.

Anyone involved in a complaint will not be responsible for deciding how that complaint is handled.
